# Material GRUB 1.1.0

The questions the installer asks about GRUB now appear on **Material Boot's new installer screen** (material-boot-core 1.1.0), in colour, with a preview of the look you are choosing. Your menu itself is unchanged.

## What's new
- **Asked on the screen:** the **screen resolution** (detected automatically, picked from a list, or typed), the raw entry name line, the countdown bar, the timeout in seconds, and whether to also install the graphical editor. Each question has a short explanation under it.
- **Nothing is asked twice.** Whatever you already gave as an option (`--grub-res 1920x1080`, say) is not asked again, and an update keeps your settings and asks nothing new about GRUB.
- **Still scriptable.** Every answer is also an option, and the summary at the end prints the exact command that does the same without the screen.

## Good to know
- This release only adds the questions; it works with material-boot-core 1.0.x too (the questions are simply not shown, and the options work as before).
- The screen itself is part of **material-boot-core 1.1.0**: download the bundle from that release.

## How we tested it (and what we didn't)
The screen was driven with the keyboard in a Fedora container: the list style, the countdown bar off and a 7-second timeout were chosen, GRUB installed with exactly those settings, and removal put `grub.cfg` back as it was. The questions are checked against the real manifest on every change.

**Not tested yet:** a real computer, other terminals than xterm-compatible ones, and immutable systems.
